Here is a summary of the options available to monetize your residential EV charging through Canada's Clean Fuel Regulations (CFR).

---

### 1. Club Roulez Électrique
The most popular program in Quebec, offering great hardware flexibility. It also has the best expected payout in most provinces. 

*   **Return:** **$0.13/kWh** in Quebec, Ontario and British Columbia (variable in other provinces).
*   **Compatible Chargers:** Tesla, Wallbox, EVDuty, Autel, Leviton, other OCPP-compliant chargers, and specialized chargers sold by Roulez Électrique.
*   **Payment:** Once per year.
*   **Hardware:** The user provides their own charger.
*   **Link:** [Join Club Roulez Électrique](https://roulezelectrique.club/r/SH8PASUP?lang=en)

---

### 2. ChargeLab (Home Charging Rewards)
A straightforward program that rewards users with gift cards.

*   **Return:** **$0.10/kWh**.
*   **Compatible Chargers:** Any charger compatible with the OCPP protocol.
*   **Payment:** 4 times per year (Quarterly), issued as **gift cards**.
*   **Hardware:** The user provides their own charger.
*   **Link:** [Sign up for ChargeLab Rewards](https://chargelab.co/home-charging-rewards-canada)

---

### 3. Club Grizzl-E
Ideal for those who do not yet own a smart charger and want a low-cost model.

*   **Return:** Progressive scale from **$0.03/kWh to $0.10/kWh**.
*   **Compatible Chargers:** Grizzl-E, as well as a wide range of other smart models.
*   **Payment:** Once your balance reaches **$10**.
*   **Hardware:** The program **provides the charger for free** (under certain conditions and with a deposit) for base models.
*   **Link:** [Join Club Grizzl-E](https://club.grizzl-e.com/en)

---

### 4. SWTCH Home Charging
Similar to the Grizzl-E model, but with a faster rate progression.

*   **Return:** Progressive scale from **$0.03/kWh to $0.10/kWh** (progression is faster than Grizzl-E).
*   **Compatible Chargers:** SWTCH branded chargers only.
*   **Payment:** Once your balance reaches **$100**.
*   **Hardware:** The program **provides the charger for free** (under certain conditions and with a deposit).
*   **Link:** [Sign up for SWTCH Home](https://shop.swtchenergy.com/)

---


> **Important Reminder:** You can only register your charger with **one** of these programs. Choose the one that best matches your current equipment or your hardware needs.

**Disclaimer:** The programs are in rapid evolution. Refer to their websites to get the current information. Other programs also exists.
