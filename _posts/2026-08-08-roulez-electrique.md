###  How to Set Up Your Account

Follow these steps to ensure your account is configured correctly to participate:

### 1. Confirm that you are eligible and understand the program
* Go on the [Club Roulez Électrique Portal](https://roulezelectrique.club/) and confirm that you understand how it works.
* Confirm that your charger is eligible for the program and that it can reliably connect to WiFi.
* Remember, federal law only allows a charger to be registered with **one** program. You cannot connect it to multiple programs in the same year.

#### 2. Sign Up & Verify
Click the referral link above and create your account using your email address. 
If you use my refferal link, I will be your ambassador and you will be able to contact me in the app if you need my help. Please consider that I will not be able to support you in person, so if you have a complex setup, or you are not very technical, it may be better to try to find someone locally to help you. 


#### 3. Register Your Charger in the Portal
Log in to the [Club Roulez Électrique Portal](https://roulezelectrique.club/).
*   Navigate to the **My Equipment** section.
*   Click **Add a charger**.
*   Select the type of charger that you have




#### 4. Plug In and Earn
Charge your car and confirm that after a charge, you see it on the website.
